# From Permission to Admission

## An Open Interoperability Model for Autonomous Systems in Physical Environments

Public white-paper outline for technical review. This outline is descriptive and does not make novelty, adoption, or patentability claims.

1. **Problem** — Physical environments need to communicate operating requirements to autonomous systems they do not own or control.
2. **Why identity/authorization alone is insufficient** — Identity says who is requesting an action; it does not demonstrate that the current machine state satisfies local conditions.
3. **Place-defined requirements** — Machine-readable requirements, action families, spaces, context, and inherited policy.
4. **Conformance** — Deterministic plans and embodiment-appropriate proof mechanisms.
5. **Evidence** — Evidence bundles, binding to machine/place/plan state, assurance levels, freshness, and integrity.
6. **Admission profiles** — Spatially scoped `ADMITTED`, `DEGRADED`, and `DENIED` outcomes.
7. **Degraded operation** — Explicit restrictions and fail-closed handling of essential requirements.
8. **Spatial transitions** — Comparing destination requirements with prior guarantees.
9. **Selective requalification** — Reusing sufficient evidence and testing only unresolved guarantees.
10. **Security and trust** — Enforcement points, runtime integrity, infrastructure, hardware, observation, and known limitations.
11. **Interoperability model** — Core, Conformance, and Admission surfaces; independent implementations; vendor-neutral exchange.
12. **Open questions** — Discovery, identity/PKI, attestation, geometry, conflict handling, certification, and multi-vendor testing.
13. **Call for technical review** — Reproduction of the demos, schema feedback, terminology review, and independent implementation reports.
